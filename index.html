About

Somewhere between a CS degree and a habit of building things.

The non-resume version.

I'm a final-year CS (AI/ML) student who somehow ended up running a handful of real, deployed systems off free-tier infrastructure and pure stubbornness. It started as "something concrete for the resume" and turned into an actual habit — I'm more likely to spend a weekend building an open-source project than studying for an exam.

Most of what I build starts as a half-baked idea that eats my weekdays and evolves into a solid project. I lean hard on measuring things — RAGAS scores, latency numbers, false-positive rates — because quality should be something you can show, not just claim.

Outside of that: way too many side-project ideas lying around in my notes.

DevPulse

Webhook delivery that survives crashes, 5xx errors, and duplicate sends.

A self-hosted reliable webhook delivery system. Events are committed to PostgreSQL with a transactional outbox, published through Redpanda, and delivered by a worker as HMAC-signed requests, with durable retries, idempotent ingestion, tenant isolation, and SSRF-safe destination validation. Verified locally with a deterministic receiver that fails once, then recovers.
